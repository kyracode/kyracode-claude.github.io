The Short Version

I'm a filmmaker and writer based in [Your Location]. My work spans short film, documentary, fiction writing, and long-form critical essays on film and economics. I'm drawn to the intersections — between personal and political, cinematic and literary, creative and analytical.

I studied [Your Field] at [Your Institution], where I made my first short films and fell in love with the mechanics of storytelling. Since then I've continued making films independently while building a parallel life as a writer and thinker.

What I Do

On this site you'll find my film portfolio — short and documentary work exploring memory, place, and human relationships. You'll also find my writing on finance and economics, where I try to make complex systems legible and interesting to a broad audience.

I also write book and film reviews — not in the star-rating listicle sense, but in the deeper sense of trying to understand why a work does what it does, and whether it does it well.

Background

I grew up between [Place 1] and [Place 2], which gave me a slightly fragmented sense of home that's probably central to a lot of my work. I've been writing since I could hold a pen and filming since I could hold a camera — which, it turns out, are pretty similar impulses.
