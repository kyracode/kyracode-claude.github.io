Short Film · 2025

The Space Between Frames

Dir. Kyra Jolly  ·  2025  ·  18 min

A meditation on memory and urban space. Shot over four months in London, Tbilisi, and Lisbon, the film follows a woman returning to places she once called home — only to find they no longer recognise her. A quiet, intimate exploration of belonging, displacement, and the stories our cities tell about who we used to be.

Documentary · 2025

Margins of the Market

Dir. Kyra Jolly  ·  2025  ·  32 min

A documentary portrait of informal economy workers in three European cities. Following a flower seller in Warsaw, a street musician in Athens, and a vintage dealer in Prague, the film asks what economic statistics can never capture — the texture, dignity, and ingenuity of lives lived at the edges of formal systems.

Short Film · 2024

Tides

Dir. Kyra Jolly  ·  2024  ·  12 min

Two sisters return to their childhood coastal town after their grandmother's death. Between clearing out the house and making small talk with neighbours who remember them too well, old fractures begin to surface. A film about the impossible weight of family history and the places that hold it.

The Cartographer's Daughter

Dir. Kyra Jolly  ·  2026  ·  In Development
